Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Easy Workflow Pro ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. We collect minimal personal data necessary to provide and secure our license activation services:

  • Account Information: When you purchase or activate a license, we collect your email address, purchase details, and license activation key.
  • Hardware ID: The extension generates a hashed identifier (non-reversible string) corresponding to your computer hardware components. This is used solely to verify active device limits and validate your license key.
  • Local Data: We do NOT collect or access your After Effects projects, layers, compositions, footage, assets, or local scripts. All extension rendering, keyframe generations, and file saving operations run entirely locally on your workstation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information only for the following business purposes:

  • To activate and validate your license keys when you open the extension panel inside Adobe After Effects.
  • To prevent license misuse, piracy, or concurrent running violations.
  • To provide customer support and troubleshoot installation issues.
  • To send update notifications, safety alerts, or customer advisories.

3. Third-Party Data Handlers

We utilize trusted third-party providers to secure license verification and process payments:

  • Firebase: Used for secure storage of database records containing user activation logs (email, license key, and hashed hardware identifier).
  • Gumroad / Cashfree: Payments are processed securely via our merchant processors. We do not store or see credit card details or bank account credentials.

4. Data Retention

We retain your email and license metadata as long as your license is active. If you wish to delete your account or reset your license activations, you can submit a request directly to our support email.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical measures to protect database records from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.
